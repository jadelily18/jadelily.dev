As of the time of writing this, I am a 22-year-old trans woman living in western Kentucky. You may know me from my work at [Modrinth](https://modrinth.com/) as a Content Moderator or for my presence in our community Discord server, which I moderate and partially manage.

This post is just going to be me rambling about myself and the things I like.

TL;DR: I’m gay and autistic.

## My Identity

As previously stated, I’m queer and trans. Being queer is a fundamental part of who I am and likely always will be, even if I’m still not sure of the right label for my sexuality at this point; queer works! Being trans is also a fundamental part of my identity, although I’d imagine this is the case for most other trans people as well.

Weirdly enough, being a furry is also a large part of my identity now, going as far as my two main fursonas being direct representations of myself. Maybe I’ll write more about them in the future. 👀

## My Interests

I have a wide range of interests, but I’m just going to talk about a few of the main ones for this post! This is a bit ranty, but that’s kinda the intention!

### Music

I’ve always loved music, from singing country songs on the radio with my mom on our way to elementary school to screaming along to Linkin Park lyrics at their Nashville concert in August of 2025.

The aforementioned Linkin Park has been my favorite band since middle school, so most of this section is going to be about them. :3

#### The Little Things Give You Away

For a long while now, my favorite song has been the closer of Linkin Park’s 2007 album Minutes to Midnight, [The Little Things Give You Away](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Gs0t8LXH6lw), a melancholic yet beautiful end to their third album in the form of a protest song against then-President George W. Bush’s handling of Hurricane Katrina.

I have… quite a lot more to say about this song, but that’s out of scope for this post.

#### A Thousand Suns

Unsurprisingly, my favorite album is also by Linkin Park; that being their 2010 album A Thousand Suns, a concept album about nuclear war.

A Thousand Suns evolves their sound even further than their previous albums and continues the trend of political messaging and advocacy from their previous album, Minutes to Midnight, going so far as to intertwine famous anti-fascist and revolutionary speeches—like those of Mario Savio and Martin Luther King, Jr.—within the music, alongside samples of haunting quotes from the likes of J. Robert Oppenheimer.

As is incredibly unsurprising, I also have quite a lot to say about this album, but that is also out of scope for this post; I highly recommend watching Simply Joe Nathan’s video on the album; it gives incredible insight into it and is genuinely a really good watch.

#### Other Music

Linkin Park isn’t the only music I listen to; I also listen to quite a bit of Twenty One Pilots, Green Day, Three Days Grace, Papa Roach, and many, many more.

Recently, I’ve been listening to quite a bit of Sleep Token, which feels very, very similar to Linkin Park in some ways, particularly the variety of sound and the emotional substance. I think my favorite Sleep Token song would currently be Jericho from their second EP, Two.

### Gaming

Gaming has been my main interest for as long as I remember, dating back to Minecraft: Pocket Edition and Halo: Combat Evolved Anniversary in 2011 and even earlier if you count Wii Sports or Lego Island.

#### Minecraft

Since then, Minecraft has been a game I’ve kept coming back to; I distinctly remember playing Minecraft: Pocket Edition Lite on my mom’s work iPad, playing Xbox 360 Edition on an Xbox in my friend’s basement, finally playing it on my own that Christmas, and eventually playing Java Edition for the first time in 1.3.2 on my dad’s computer.

Countless hours were spent on Survival Games, Capture the Flag, and minigames on The Hive or Mineplex. Unfortunately, I can’t recall the name, but I remember there being a specific Capture the Flag server that I loved to play on.

Eventually, I found out what modding was, resulting in countless failed attempts at getting Forge working. Much later, I remember playing the Weather mod and Too Much TNT on my laptop in the back of my friend’s mom’s car.

Later, I would go on to [start modding the game myself](https://modrinth.com/@jade) and, of course, eventually land a job at Modrinth.
